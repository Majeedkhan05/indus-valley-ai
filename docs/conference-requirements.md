### Honest fit assessment
ICLR is a top-tier machine-learning venue with a very low acceptance rate. Submissions
compete on **methodological novelty in ML**. A well-engineered domain RAG system with a
completed benchmark — which is the realistic ceiling here in four weeks — is **unlikely to
be competitive in the main track**.

Realistic options at that venue:
- an **ICLR workshop** (deadlines usually later, scope friendlier to applied/domain work);
- treat the main-track deadline as a forcing function, and submit the finished work to a
  digital-humanities / computational-archaeology venue instead.

Do not submit an under-evaluated system to a main track. A rejection with no experiments
costs more than a later submission with real numbers.
